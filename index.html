AI decision support for negotiation and national security · week 1

Who is using AI to prepare decisions of state

Bottom line. Governments have moved fastest on general-purpose assistants and on military planning and wargaming tools. AI built for negotiation or for public investment choices is mostly prototypes and private-sector analogs, and the State Department is the one foreign ministry we found publicly steering its assistant toward negotiation and policy analysis. Against our framing, nothing covers the after-talks phase or budget-wide stress tests. Most Western deployments rest on a handful of U.S. model developers and integrators, and France and Japan are already hedging with domestic models.
